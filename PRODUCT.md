# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Streamers are the primary users and the main distribution channel: they play live to show off their history knowledge, captured beside a webcam into a 720p to 1080p stream, and need a game simple enough to pick up mid-stream. Viewers are the secondary users: they watch on phones and TVs, then play the same puzzle on their own phones.

## Product Purpose

History Links makes chronology a competitive sport: who was alive at the same time as whom. The player connects two famous people through others whose lives overlapped, in as few people as possible. Success is completed games, shared reveals, and viewers who become players.

## Positioning

The game scores the overlap itself. Every placement reveals when a person lived and who they shared years with, on daily puzzles shared by everyone. Three ways to play make it the difficulty scale: Easy arranges name cards, Medium fills letter-count blanks, and Hard is free typing scored like golf against par.

## Operating Context

- Three daily puzzles per UTC date, one per style, all with the same number; Hard's pairs are closer early in the week. One scored attempt per browser per style, past puzzles replayed as practice; free play in a chosen style.
- Streamers play on desktop with the game window captured into a stream; viewers play on phones, often one-handed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- People only, as targets and as links.
- Data only from Wikipedia and Wikidata, including portraits.
- No accounts: play and share without signing up.
- Lifespans stay hidden until a person is placed.
- Par, hints and the Easy and Medium puzzles come from a committed roster of well-known people.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: History Links.
- One-liner: chronology awareness as a competitive sport.

## Evidence on Hand

- Portraits and short descriptions come from Wikipedia page images and Wikidata; some people have no portrait.
- No testimonials, player counts or press exist yet; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- The reveal is the payoff: design every placement around the moment a life is shown.
- Readable on a 720p stream and playable one-handed on a phone.
- The board tells the truth: positions and lengths are real years.
